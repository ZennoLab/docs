## Demo Version Limitations

1. The program works for 14 days
2. Only one execution thread is available
3. Proxy usage is disabled
4. Captchas can only be entered manually (you can't use services like [CapMonster.cloud](https://capmonster.cloud/ "https://capmonster.cloud/"))
5. If you did not enter your email when installing the demo, you cannot transfer projects to other users: after the demo expires, your projects will become unusable.
6. The demo version is not a substitute for the Lite license: while using the demo, you can test features and create templates, but debugging (running a template in ProjectMaker) and executing templates in ZennoPoster/ZennoDroid will trigger a timer that tracks automation runtime. When this timer reaches 10 minutes, you’ll be prompted to enter a captcha manually to continue.
7. You can't use [❗→ bat files to launch projects](/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/1940979747 "/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/1940979747").

If projects made in the demo version stop opening in a new demo or in the licensed version, you can send them to support@zennolab.com from your license email, and we'll help restore access to the projects for your current license.

## How to extend your demo period

If your demo license has expired, you’ll be able to try again in 16 days.
