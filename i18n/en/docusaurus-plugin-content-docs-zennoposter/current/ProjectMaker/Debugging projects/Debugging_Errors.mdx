## Description
During template development and testing, use the [log window](https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/725352532 "https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/725352532") to find and fix errors. It records the results of all successful and failed actions.

![image-20210222-193345](./assets/Debugging_Errors/Debugging_Errors_pic1.png)

### Tools for working with errors:
- **Color coding.** Entries for failed actions are highlighted in red.  
- **Quick navigation.** Double-click a log entry to automatically locate the problematic action and center the project workspace on it.  
- **Copying IDs.** Right-click an entry to copy the action's ID. This option is also available in the ZennoPoster log.

### Search
You can easily [find a specific action by its ID](https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/724566074 "https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/724566074"):  

![image-20210222-193057](./assets/Debugging_Errors/Debugging_Errors_pic2.png)

Once you have found the action that caused the error, you can [adjust its parameters](https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/494731280 "https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/494731280") and [resume project execution from the current action](https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/494731265/1#%D0%A1-%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B0 "https://zennolab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RU/pages/494731265/1#%D0%A1-%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B0").
