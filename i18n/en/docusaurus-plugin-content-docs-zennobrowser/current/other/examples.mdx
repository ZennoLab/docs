## **Product usage examples**

**ZennoBrowser** will help you with tasks related to creating accounts on any service (crypto services, email clients, hosting, forums, marketplaces, and so on). It is suitable for any workflow involving mass actions.

Our anti-detect browser lets you perform routine tasks manually. Integration with ZennoPoster opens up automation opportunities and allows you to scale to the next level. So, manual actions in ZennoBrowser are intended for solving specific tasks, while automation gives you the tools to manage processes when dealing with larger volumes.

You can find specific examples in our contest sections [on the forum](https://zenno.club/discussion/forums/poleznye-stati-istorii-uspexa.154). All the solutions offered by our community members can be used both manually and automatically. The choice depends entirely on the volume and results you expect.
