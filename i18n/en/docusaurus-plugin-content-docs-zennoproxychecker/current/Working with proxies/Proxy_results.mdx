To continuously receive proxies required for your projects and programs, you need to configure proxy distribution. ProxyChecker allows you to distribute proxies to a local or network resource at a specified interval or upon request. 

You can read about how to configure the *Distribution* tab [here](https://docs.zennolab.com/zennoproxychecker/Program%20interface/Results).
