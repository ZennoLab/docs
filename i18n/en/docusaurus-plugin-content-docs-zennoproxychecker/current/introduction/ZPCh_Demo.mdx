## **ZennoProxyChecker Demo Limitations**

 - The program works for 14 days.
 - The number of ProxyChecker threads is 100.
 - Ports are not displayed for the found proxies.
 - The obtained proxies cannot be saved to a file.
 - Proxies are not saved when the program is closed.
 - Proxies cannot be saved in the live proxies tab.

## **How to Extend the Demo Period**

If your Demo license has expired, you will be able to try again in 16 days.
