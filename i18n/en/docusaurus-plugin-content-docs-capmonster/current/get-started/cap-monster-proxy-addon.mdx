## Description.  
In CapMonster, you can use built-in proxies from our partner **hit-proxy.com** to solve ReCaptcha.  

When you purchase the *CapMonsterProxyAddon* module, you receive **proxies with unlimited traffic and support for up to 1,000 simultaneous connections**.

The *CapMonsterProxyAddon* module is purchased in the [Personal Account](https://userarea.zennolab.com/lk/userarea/UserCustomer.aspx) for a period of one month.  

:::warning **You need to pay for the addon separately for each CapMonster license.**
:::

The addon works отлично with recognition methods via *SiteKeyAddon for ReCaptcha*. The proxies are bound to the IP address of the active CapMonster license.  

### Connection.  
You can connect the purchased module in the CapMonster settings on the *ReCaptchaSitekey Addon* tab. To do this, select *Built-in* as the **Proxy acquisition method**.  

![Connection](./assets/CapMonsterProxyAddon.png) 

After that, *RecaptchaSitekey Addon* will solve Recaptcha using proxies from *CapMonster ProxyAddon*.
